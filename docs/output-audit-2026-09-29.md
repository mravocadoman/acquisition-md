## What scores, every time

The posts that reach 11–12 are all the same kind: **two of his real ad-account numbers next to each other, and the gap between them is the post.** 36x on €1,000 vs 13x on €75K/month. €0.38 a lead vs 2% bought. Meta 68 sales vs Shopify 54. Nothing else in the corpus gets there — not the stories, not the AI-stack posts, not the magnets.

## Failure patterns (all three audits found the same five)

1. **One house template, visible.** Twist hook → "Context:" → arrow list → "the honest trade-off" → aphorism couplet → "What is the best X you ever Y?" → rotating sign-off. "Honest" used as a structural word in 11 of 35 archive posts. Em dashes banned, so spaced hyphens appear at exactly em-dash frequency (27 in nine machine posts). Read one post, fine; read the week, it's a machine.
2. **The best line is in the first comment.** Nine archive posts, three Episode 1 posts, three machine posts: the sharpest sentence was written last and filed under the post. ("Structure moved double digits, cleverness moved single digits." "Two campaigns wearing one dashboard." "Writes none of my posts and publishes all of them.")
3. **Numbers that undercut themselves.** €0.38 × 2% × €250 on €1,175 spend ≈ 8.5x — the campaign *won*, and the post frames it as the lie exposed. "12 tries, 11 failures" becomes "about a dozen, most failed" four paragraphs later. "Twelve years later" from 2016. 5% reply rate is per-open; per-sent it is 2.6%. The exact audience he wants will do the arithmetic in the comments.
4. **Serial dependency.** Four of five Episode 1 posts cannot be read cold ("Yesterday: Meta said 68…", "Week one of the handover, closed."). Fine for a week that airs; fatal for one that slipped twice. The format made approval a 12-week commitment instead of a one-post decision.
5. **Length creep on redraft.** Every second draft came back longer: Cheap Leads 184→335, Coach 355→415, Kill-idea 277→370, Nomad 261→566. Reflection was added, nothing was cut.

Machine-specific: the same anecdote twice in one week (Honolulu, 24 and 26 Aug), a post asserting the severity of his depression that no model can vouch for, "Context:" and "The generalisable bit:" shipping in the one post that actually published. **Verdict: not safe to run unattended**, and a single-pass human read will wave every one of those through.

Magnet-specific: every magnet post is a contents list plus a gate. Four still carry "Comment REVIEW / LEADS / NATIVE / SHIP". The hosted TEAM and CAROUSEL assets still carry comment chips in their own headers.

## Why nothing shipped (the honest version)

Not craft — the floor is high, almost nothing is unpublishable. **It is a decision problem.** 44 of 77 archive pages are titles with no body; the calendar minted topics twice as fast as anything got written and re-minted the same titles a dozen times. Six stories were redrafted two or three times instead of posted once. Approving Episode 1 meant signing up to twelve weeks and a job-two system that did not exist. Approving the magnets meant publishing gates his own rules had banned. The queue asked him to sign his name to a plan he had half-abandoned, in a voice that is 80% his and 20% scaffolding, and the one post that shipped, shipped because nobody asked him to tick it.

## What the platform rewards now (Sep 2026)

Two sources, July and September 2026 updates: document carousels ≈ 6.6% engagement, highest of any format, 8–12 slides; saves weigh 5x a like; dwell over 61 s → 15.6% engagement vs 1.2% under 3 s; first 30–60 minutes decide distribution; 2–5 posts a week; expertise posts +40%; AI-sounding content without original perspective ≈ 2.8x less reach. **External links in the body ≈ 60% less reach — and the "link in first comment" workaround was patched in early 2026 and now reads as bridge behaviour.** Comment-gating is listed spam. That retires the 5 Sep rule too: the kit URL goes on the carousel's last slide and in the profile, and the ask is "DM me" or nothing.
