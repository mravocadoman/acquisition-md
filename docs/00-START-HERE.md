## Settled — do not re-open

1. **Lead magnets lead** (29 Sep late; mix 12 Oct). Three of four posts a week carry a kit; the claim in line one is impressive and literally true; the mechanism is in the post; the kit is the artifact itself (prompts, code, schema), never a PDF about it. The first Batch One of "interesting value posts" was rejected in one line.
2. **The ask is in the text and on the last slide** (29 Sep late): "Comment KEYWORD and I will send it to you. Free, no email, no form." No URL in body or comments, ever. Manual fulfilment on LinkedIn.
3. **Kit before post.** A lead magnet is never written for a kit that does not exist as a hosted page. When the material exists, the writing session builds the kit first (as ADS was built from the live task prompts).
4. **Every non-story post ships a code-drawn data carousel** drawn by `deck2.py`: **slide 1 is a drawn visual hook** (a picture of the post's number: tiles, a toggle, bars, a clock ring, two circles, a gauge, code-to-slide) over a two-tone headline, never a text-only cover (30 Sep afternoon); **a row of tool chips under the art when the post names tools** (30 Sep late), each carrying **the tool's official mark in its official colour on a white pill** (12 Oct, `brand_icons.py` v2; Simple Icons path data, Font Awesome Free for LinkedIn, never drawn by hand) or a generic pictogram where no mark is published (Zernio, Excel, matplotlib, Canva, Printify, Email / Lead form / PDF), plus the tool's name, only tools the post names, at most five, a struck chip for "not this one"; **at most 32 words on any slide** (40 until 1 Oct), one idea per slide, three or four items, big type; **no reader text under 30pt and every card on one grid** (1 Oct); the generator measures every line and refuses to save a deck with clipped text, overlapping text or a slide over the word limit. AI images and AI-drawn charts stay banned.
5. **Standalone posts, four a week, written two weeks at a time, one batch read.** The serial format and the daily approval tick are retired.
6. **The n8n machine writer is retired.** An approved entry with no FINAL POST asks for a voice note instead of inventing one.
7. **Numbers must survive their own arithmetic**, and the slides carry the same numbers.
8. **No story or kit reused within 60 days.**
9. **The system is `acquisition.md`; the wordmark on slides, outros and kit pages is `rihardszeila.com`** (30 Sep evening). Instagram is not a placement. Latvian is paused until he supplies samples.
10. **The print-on-demand business is never named** and its theme never hinted at: text, slides, kits, screenshots.
11. **Claude never approves.** Nothing posts without Rihards's tick. Re-verified 29 Sep and again on 12 Oct when the batch was re-dated (every row stayed `Approved = No`).
12. **Publish after every n8n change** (the manual hosting helpers excepted; they are never published).
13. **The Monthly Content Planner, the tip scrapers and the two "Martin" clones are dead weight** — all five are unpublished as of 30 Sep. Do not republish them.
14. **The line count in the CAROUSEL post is the real one** (726 since 30 Sep late). It is repeated in the post, the cover, Notion, the kit page, the artifact and the docs; change all or none, and keep new art helpers and the brand-mark code out of `deck2.py` (they live in `art_extra.py` and `brand_icons.py`).
15. **Navy only** (12 Oct: "let's keep the dark background on posts"). Every deck is `Deck(slug, OUT, "navy")`; the light theme stays in the code for the specimen and is used by no post; the alternation rule is retired. Cyan and navy ink only (a cyan background was rejected on 30 Sep, a third blue on 1 Oct); the brand marks' own colours on white pills are the one exception, and only because they are the brands'. The white light theme of v2.4 (never cream) is kept correct in `deck2.py` but not shipped.
16. **Three kits and one opinion post a week** (12 Oct, prompt v17, v18 the same evening; it was two kits + opinion + numbers/story from 30 Sep to 12 Oct). A NUMBERS post or a STORY fills a kit slot only when no kit is ready that week, and the Sunday report says so. Batch One: six kits and two opinion posts, 19–30 Oct; the $480 coach story is archived, not deleted.
17. **The first comment of every kit post ends with the Featured line** ("If you would rather not wait, it is pinned in the Featured section of my profile"), so nobody waits on DMs. Needs the six kit links pinned before Mon 19 Oct, or the sentence comes out of all six comments.
18. **Metrics are typed by hand into Notion** (Impressions / Reactions / Comments / Saves / Metrics as of) and mirrored to `content_log` by the Sunday session; no LinkedIn API. The Friday nudge lists the rows still empty.
19. **Motion is real screens or nothing** (12 Oct). Code-drawn motion is a prototype for transitions only (`carousel-motion.py`); a visual-hook clip is Rihards's own screen recording of the real tool, 6 to 12 seconds, cover frame first and last. AI (the Higgsfield connector, no credits) may generate ambience, never a UI, a chart, a figure or a face. Nothing ships until he says "shoot" and records the shot list.
20. **Secrets never travel through chat or a workflow payload** (12 Oct, rule 15 in `content-rules-standing.md`). Keys that have appeared in chat or sit in old n8n versions are treated as compromised until rotated; a session never restates one.
