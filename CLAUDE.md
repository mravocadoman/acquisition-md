## What this is

A lead-magnet posting system for Rihards's LinkedIn. Claude writes the posts, draws the carousels in code and builds the kits; Rihards reads a batch once and says go; n8n packages and Zernio publishes; he answers the keyword comments by hand. Nothing posts without his tick in Notion. **Claude never ticks Approved, never publishes an n8n workflow, never pastes a credential anywhere, never automates LinkedIn browsing on his account, and never names the print-on-demand business** (it is only ever "a print-on-demand ecommerce project"; its niche, theme and franchise are never revealed in text, on a slide, in a kit, in a screenshot or in this repo). The LinkedIn rule exists because a research agent's automated searches got search paused on his account (Oct 2026): research LinkedIn by web search or his own exports.

## Rules that bite in code (the full set is docs/content-rules-standing.md)

1. Navy only: every deck is `Deck(slug, OUT, "navy")`. Cyan and navy ink; the amber gate box is the one third colour; the brand marks' own colours on white pills are the only other exception.
2. At most 32 words on a slide (`MAX_WORDS`), nothing a reader is meant to read under 30pt, every card on the one grid (`TOP` / `HEAD` / `GRID`). `save()` refuses a deck that breaks this: cut copy, never touch the layout constants or pass your own sizes.
3. Slide 1 is a drawn visual hook (an `art_*` helper) over a two-tone headline, with tool chips only for tools the post text names, at most five, `("Canva", True)` for a struck "not this one".
4. The numbers on the slides equal the numbers in the post's facts list; if one changes, both change.
5. Every kit post ends with "Comment KEYWORD and I will send it to you. Free, no email, no form." and its first comment with "Sent by hand, give me an hour. If you would rather not wait, it is pinned in the Featured section of my profile." No URL in a body or a comment, ever. The kit must exist at `rihardszeila.com/kits/<path>.html` before the post is written, and the kit must ask for the same KEYWORD as the post (the START kit said "SHIP" until 20 Oct).
6. Posts are 150–250 words (opinion 150–230, story under 260), hyphens never em dashes, no relative dates ("last Monday", "a week in"): write the date or a durable phrase, because batches slip.
7. Weekly mix since 12 Oct: three kit posts + one opinion post; a numbers or story post only when no kit is ready.
8. The wordmark on slides, outros and kit pages is `rihardszeila.com`; `acquisition.md` is the name of the system and never a link or footer.
9. Keys and passwords live in the environment or in n8n credentials, never in a file in this repo, never in a chat message, never in a workflow payload. Several keys that appeared in earlier chats or sit in old n8n versions are on the rotation list (`docs/post-generator-approval-runbook.md`, open item 9).

## What is easier from a local machine than from the cloud session

The cloud session could not reach Supabase, n8n or Notion from its shell, so hosting a 15 KB PDF meant pasting base64 through chat in parts and checking md5s. From a laptop with the Supabase service key in the environment, `hosted_assets` can be written directly (REST upsert on `path`, body base64 for binaries, UTF-8 text with `encoding = 'utf8'`, then `select md5(decode(body,'base64'))` to verify), and covers can be rendered locally (`pdftoppm`) and upserted as `carousels/<slug>-cover.jpg` (800×800 JPEG) instead of going through PDF.co. Keep the same verification habit: md5 of the raw bytes, then fetch the URL once with `?v=<n>` (15-minute cache). Video and screen recordings also belong here (ffmpeg). Video posts are wired in since 14 Oct: put a `=== VIDEO URL ===` section in the Notion body directly before `=== MAGNET URL ===`, and the packager and publisher take it from there. The first approved video post is the first end-to-end test of Zernio accepting a video from a Supabase URL, so watch that run.
